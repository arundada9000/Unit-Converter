Privacy Policy

At All Unit Converter, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information this site may collect, how it is used, and the choices you have — including the third-party advertising cookies served by Google AdSense. It was last updated in 2026.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal information. The converters themselves run entirely in your browser, so the values you type are never transmitted to or stored by us. The types of information that may be collected include:

  • Non-personal usage data (for example, page views and time spent on the site).
  • Basic contact information (such as an email address) if you choose to provide it through the contact page.
  • Technical data such as browser type and approximate region, collected automatically by the services described below.

Cookies and Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.

  • Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and other sites across the internet.
  • Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • For more control over personalised advertising, visit https://adssettings.google.com.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at https://www.aboutads.info and https://www.networkadvertising.org.

Because this site is a Progressive Web App, it may also store static files in your browser's local cache so the tools keep working offline. This cache contains no personal data and is removed when you clear your browser's site data.

Any information collected is used only to improve the site's functionality and user experience. Specifically, it may be used to:

Third-Party Services
