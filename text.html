Text Styling

Simple text styler, use buttons or simply copy paste.

A text formatter that never uploads your words

Paste text into this tool to fix casing, apply bold or italic styling, and see an instant count of what's in it — words, letters, lines, sentences, numbers and more. It's built for the quick jobs a full word processor makes heavy work of: fixing a heading's capitalisation, prepping a snippet for a post, or checking the length of a bio. Everything runs in your browser, so the text you paste never leaves your device.

What you can do with it

  • Change case — UPPERCASE, lowercase, title case (Capitalize) or sentence case from one dropdown.
  • Style while you write — toggle bold and italic on the textarea to preview how a sentence scans before you copy it out.
  • Copy and paste — one-click copy to the clipboard, or paste straight in and start editing.
  • Read the stats — a live counter breaks down words, letters, lines, sentences, spaces, numbers and symbols as you type.

Why keep it in the browser

  • Zero latency: no server round-trip, so every change is instant even on a slow connection.
  • Privacy by design: nothing is stored, tracked or indexed — your essays, emails and drafts stay on your machine.
  • Clean output: pasting from a PDF or website drags in invisible formatting; this tool works on plain text so you're left with what you see.

Case options at a glance

Option What it does Example
UPPERCASE Every character to capitals — for headers and warnings. THE QUICK BROWN FOX.
lowercase Every character to lowercase — cleans up erratic typing. the quick brown fox.
Capitalize First letter of every word — title case for headings. The Quick Brown Fox.
Sentence case First letter of each sentence only. The quick brown fox.

What the live counter actually tells you

The stats bar is more useful than it looks. Word count sizes a bio or a tweet; letter count flags a password-style field; the sentence count reveals whether you're writing long run-ons; and the symbol count catches stray em-dashes or curly quotes that a platform might reject. Social-media platforms and job-portal character limits are the most common reason people reach for a counter like this.

Text-tool questions that keep coming up

Does this tool auto-save my work?

No — deliberately. Nothing is stored anywhere, so if you refresh or close the tab, the text is gone. Copy your finished text to the clipboard before leaving.

Can I use it for social media posts?

Yes — it's built for drafting long-form posts, threads and article intros where casing and character counts need to be right before publishing.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Like the rest of this site, the tool is a Progressive Web App — once the page has loaded, it keeps working with no connection, because all the logic is local.

Will it strip formatting from pasted text?

The textarea works with plain text, so pasting rich content drops hidden formatting automatically — exactly what you want when a document came from a PDF or another website.
